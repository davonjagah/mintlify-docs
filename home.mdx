Transika is an institutional merchant API platform built for businesses that need to move value between USDT/USDC stablecoins and Ghana cedis (GHS). You can hold a managed GHS treasury balance (tGHS), pay Ghana mobile money (MoMo) and bank beneficiaries, and receive signed webhook events across the full OTC trade lifecycle. Whether you are a fintech, payment aggregator, or treasury desk, Transika gives you a single API to operate in the Ghana market.

## What you can build

- Exchange USDT or USDC for Ghana cedis (GHS) and vice versa via OTC trades
- Hold and reconcile a managed GHS treasury balance (tGHS)
- Pay Ghana mobile money (MoMo) and bank beneficiaries from your tGHS balance
- Maintain a roster of verified Ghana beneficiaries
- Receive signed webhook events for every stage of the OTC trade lifecycle
- Automate treasury operations and real-time settlement

## Integration steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete KYB">
    Submit your business verification documents and obtain approved KYB and product access from the Transika team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start in sandbox">
    Create a sandbox API key and point your integration at the `/sandbox/v1` base URL to test all flows without moving real funds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle responses">
    Parse successful payloads from the `data` envelope, map non-2xx status codes to your error handling logic, and process events idempotently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Swap your sandbox key for a live API key, update your base URL to the production endpoint, and monitor signed webhook events in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
